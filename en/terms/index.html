The game

Every day at midnight Paris time, Bascule publishes three dilemmas, the same for everyone. You vote once per dilemma, without an account. The dilemmas are imagined situations meant to make you think: they are neither advice nor real events.

The percentages reflect the people who voted in the app and on the website, not a representative population. It is not a poll.

Bascule is meant for people aged 13 and over.
